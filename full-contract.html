MAF STAFFING SOLUTIONS / MAF WORLDWIDE EMPLOYMENT, COMPENSATION, COMMISSION, BENEFITS, LEAVE, AND WORKER AGREEMENT This Employment, Compensation, Commission, Benefits, Leave, and Worker Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into as of the ___ day of _______________, 20, by and between: Company / Employer: MAF Staffing Solutions, also operating under or affiliated with MAF WorldWide Company Representative: Melissa Steele Company Representative Email: melissasteele462@outlook.com and Employee / Worker Name: ___________________________________________ Employee / Worker Email: ___________________________________________ Employee / Worker Phone: ___________________________________________ Employee / Worker Address: ___________________________________________ Position / Department: ___________________________________________ Start Date: ___________________________________________ Worker Classification: ☐ Employee ☐ Commission-Based Worker ☐ Sales Representative ☐ Sourcer ☐ Recruiter ☐ Director ☐ Department Head ☐ Internal Staffing Team Member ☐ Other: ___________________________________________ The Company and the Employee / Worker may be referred to individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.” --- 1. Purpose of Agreement This Agreement sets out the terms under which the Employee / Worker will perform services for MAF Staffing Solutions / MAF WorldWide. This Agreement covers: 1. Employment or worker relationship. 2. Duties and professional responsibilities. 3. Temporary salary structure. 4. Salary structure after six months in business. 5. Commission structure. 6. Commission banking and payout rules. 7. Sales, sourcer, and recruiter commission rights. 8. Probationary period. 9. Paid time off. 10. Vacation and sick leave. 11. Bereavement leave. 12. Parental leave. 13. Healthcare and benefit access. 14. Mental health and wellness reimbursement. 15. Gym membership or gym reimbursement. 16. Nutritionist benefit. 17. Tuition reimbursement. 18. Meal and snack allowances. 19. Carryover rules. 20. Separation and payout rules. 21. Confidentiality, client protection, documentation, and professional conduct. This Agreement is intended to provide a formal, professional, and organized contract for employees, commission-based workers, internal staffing team members, sales representatives, sourcers, recruiters, department heads, and directors. --- 2. Legal Review and Compliance The Employee / Worker understands that this Agreement is intended to operate alongside all applicable federal, state, and local laws. Nothing in this Agreement shall be interpreted to reduce, remove, or waive any legal right that cannot lawfully be waived. If any provision conflicts with applicable law, the law shall control, and the remaining provisions shall remain enforceable to the fullest extent permitted. The Company may update this Agreement or related policies to remain compliant with wage laws, employment laws, worker classification rules, tax rules, healthcare rules, leave laws, payroll rules, and final pay laws. --- 3. Employment or Worker Relationship The Company will determine whether the individual is classified as an employee, commission-based worker, independent contractor, or another lawful classification based on the role, duties, control, work arrangement, applicable law, and Company needs. No job title alone determines legal classification. If the Employee / Worker is classified as an employee, the Company shall handle payroll, taxes, withholdings, and employee benefits according to applicable law and Company policy. If the Employee / Worker is classified as an independent contractor or commission-based non-employee worker, the worker may be responsible for their own taxes, insurance, filings, and expenses unless otherwise required by law or agreed in writing. --- 4. Position and Duties The Employee / Worker agrees to perform all assigned duties professionally, ethically, accurately, and in the best interests of MAF Staffing Solutions / MAF WorldWide. Duties may include, but are not limited to: 1. Securing employer staffing contracts. 2. Contacting employers and clients. 3. Presenting staffing services. 4. Building employer relationships. 5. Finding candidate pools. 6. Submitting qualified candidates to recruiters. 7. Conducting candidate pre-screenings. 8. Supporting employers through the hiring process. 9. Supporting candidates or placed employees through their first 90 days. 10. Maintaining accurate candidate, client, employer, and commission records. 11. Assisting with internal staffing operations. 12. Assisting with recruitment, onboarding, communication, retention, and follow-up. 13. Supporting applicants, employees, clients, investors, and internal team members. 14. Promoting the goals, reputation, and growth of the Company. The Company may adjust duties as business needs require. --- 5. Professional Conduct The Employee / Worker agrees to conduct themselves professionally at all times when representing the Company. The Employee / Worker shall not: 1. Misrepresent Company services. 2. Misrepresent compensation, benefits, commissions, or employment opportunities. 3. Promise candidates or employers terms that have not been approved by the Company. 4. Falsify records. 5. Submit false commission claims. 6. Harass, discriminate against, threaten, or mistreat any employee, client, employer, candidate, applicant, or Company representative. 7. Disclose confidential information. 8. Damage the Company’s reputation. 9. Use Company contacts or candidate pools for unauthorized personal gain. 10. Engage in conduct that violates Company policy or applicable law. --- 6. Temporary Salary Structure for First Six Months of Business For the first six months that MAF Staffing Solutions is in business, salaries will be paid as follows: Position Category| Temporary Annual Salary Directors| $65,000 per year Department Heads| $60,000 per year All other staff| $50,000 per year These salary amounts are temporary. After MAF Staffing Solutions has been operating for six months, eligible employees will transition to the promised salary structure listed in this Agreement. --- 7. Payday Payday shall be every Thursday. If a Thursday payday falls on a bank holiday, payroll processing delay, payroll provider delay, or another operational issue, payment may be processed on the nearest available lawful payroll date. All payments are subject to applicable taxes, withholdings, deductions, and payroll rules. --- 8. Salary Structure After Six Months in Business After MAF Staffing Solutions has been in business for six months, salaries will increase to the following annual amounts for eligible employees: Position Category| Annual Salary After Six Months Directors| $215,000 per year Department Heads| $175,000 per year All other staff| $80,000 per year Eligibility for the post-six-month salary structure may depend on: 1. Continued employment or active worker status. 2. Completion of required onboarding. 3. Compliance with Company policies. 4. Satisfactory performance. 5. Business operations. 6. Payroll and legal compliance. 7. The Company actually reaching six months of business operations. --- 9. Commission Payments During First Six Months All commissions earned during the first six months of business will be banked. After the Company reaches six months of business operations, all properly earned and approved commissions will be paid out in one lump sum. Any employee, sales representative, sourcer, recruiter, commission-based worker, or eligible worker who earned commission during this period will receive their commission payout, including individuals who are no longer with the Company at the time of payout. A commission will only be considered payable if: 1. The worker performed the qualifying commission activity. 2. The work was properly documented. 3. The Company approved the commission. 4. The employer or client paid the Company. 5. The payment was not refunded, reversed, disputed, or charged back. 6. The commission complies with this Agreement and applicable law. --- 10. Annual Cost-of-Living Raises All employees will receive an annual cost-of-living raise every April 1. The cost-of-living raise shall be based on the previous year’s inflation rate. This inflation-based raise is separate from any merit-based raise. The Company may use the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics inflation calculator or another reasonable inflation source to calculate inflation adjustments. --- 11. Merit-Based Raises Merit-based raises will range from 0% to 7%. Merit-based raises will be based on overall performance reviews. Performance reviews will be completed every three months from each employee’s date of hire. Annual raise intervals will be based on each employee’s employment start date. Performance review factors may include: 1. Quality of work. 2. Attendance and reliability. 3. Client service. 4. Candidate service. 5. Employer communication. 6. Sales performance. 7. Recruiting performance. 8. Sourcing performance. 9. Documentation accuracy. 10. Compliance with Company policy. 11. Professional conduct. 12. Teamwork. 13. Productivity. 14. Contribution to Company goals. A merit-based raise is not guaranteed. The Company may award anywhere from 0% to 7% based on performance and business considerations. --- 12. Commission Structure Overview Commissions are based on funds received from employers. Commissions are tied to the salaries of employees placed with employers through MAF Staffing Solutions. No commission shall be payable unless the Company receives the applicable payment from the employer or client. Commissions may apply to: 1. Sales staff. 2. Sourcers. 3. Recruiters. 4. Workers who perform more than one eligible commission function. 5. Commission-based employees or contractors approved by the Company. --- 13. Sales Commission Sales staff who secure employer staffing contracts will receive: 1. 10% commission on all new hires connected to the qualifying employer staffing contract; and 2. A $2,500 one-time bonus for each signed employer contract. The $2,500 contract signing bonus will be payable only when: 1. The employer contract is signed. 2. The contract is accepted by the Company. 3. The contract is valid and active. 4. The sales worker is properly documented as the qualifying sales worker. 5. The contract is not cancelled, rejected, fraudulent, or invalid. --- 14. Sourcer Commission Sourcers who find pools of candidates will receive: 1. 10% commission on all new hires where the sourcer’s candidate sourcing contributed to the placement; and 2. A $25 bonus for each candidate brought to recruiters. The $25 candidate bonus is payable only if: 1. The candidate is real and contactable. 2. The candidate is submitted through the Company’s approved process. 3. The candidate is brought to a recruiter. 4. The candidate submission is documented. 5. The candidate was not already known, already submitted, or already active in the Company’s system unless otherwise approved. --- 15. Recruiter Commission Recruiters who complete pre-screenings for employers and support employees through their first 90 days will receive: 1. 30% commission on all new hires where the recruiter performed the qualifying recruiting, pre-screening, placement, and first-90-day support functions. Recruiter commission eligibility may require: 1. Candidate pre-screening documentation. 2. Employer communication records. 3. Candidate handoff records. 4. Placement confirmation. 5. First-90-day support documentation. 6. Proof that the recruiter completed required follow-up. 7. Company approval. --- 16. Commission Example If an employer hires a candidate at a salary of $50,000, and MAF Staffing Solutions receives 20% for its services, the Company would receive $10,000. From that $10,000: 1. Sales agent receives 10%, equal to $1,000. 2. Sourcer receives 10%, equal to $1,000. 3. Recruiter receives 30%, equal to $3,000. This example is for explanation only. Actual commission payment depends on: 1. The role performed. 2. Whether the worker completed the required commission activity. 3. Proper documentation. 4. Company approval. 5. Funds actually received from the employer or client. 6. Any refunds, disputes, chargebacks, nonpayment, cancellations, or offsets. 7. Compliance with this Agreement. --- 17. Uncapped and Stackable Commissions Commissions are uncapped. Commissions are stackable. If one employee or worker handles multiple parts of the process, they may earn commission for each eligible step completed. For example, if one worker secures the employer contract, sources the candidate, and performs the recruiter duties, that worker may be eligible for the sales commission, sourcer commission, and recruiter commission, provided all requirements are satisfied. All stackable commissions require: 1. Proper documentation. 2. Company approval. 3. Proof of work performed. 4. Funds received from the employer or client. 5. Compliance with this Agreement. --- 18. Commission Records and Proof The Employee / Worker must maintain accurate records to support commission claims. Records may include: 1. Employer name. 2. Client contact name. 3. Candidate name. 4. Candidate source. 5. Candidate submission date. 6. Recruiter assigned. 7. Position filled. 8. Salary of placed candidate. 9. Contract signing date. 10. Employer payment amount. 11. Proof of pre-screening. 12. Proof of first-90-day support. 13. Notes, emails, call logs, forms, or system entries requested by the Company. The Company may deny, delay, reduce, or adjust commission payments if documentation is missing, incomplete, inaccurate, disputed, duplicated, fraudulent, or not approved. --- 19. Probationary Period All employees will have a probationary period of 90 calendar days from the hire date. After completing 90 days, eligible employees will receive access to: 1. PTO. 2. Healthcare benefits. 3. Additional Company perk programs. Access to these benefits is subject to this Agreement, Company policy, plan documents, eligibility requirements, business operations, and applicable law. Paid Time Off will become accessible once MAF Staffing Solutions has been in business for six months. --- 20. Paid Time Off Eligibility Paid Time Off will become accessible after MAF Staffing Solutions has been in business for six months. PTO eligibility may also depend on completion of the 90-day probationary period and compliance with Company policy. All PTO must be requested and approved according to Company procedures unless emergency circumstances apply. The Company may deny or reschedule PTO requests based on staffing needs, client needs, business needs, blackout dates, lack of notice, abuse concerns, or other lawful reasons. --- 21. Vacation Time All eligible employees receive 10 vacation days per year. Vacation days are intended for rest, personal time, travel, family time, and other approved personal use. Vacation requests should be submitted in advance. --- 22. Sick Leave All eligible employees receive 5 sick days per year. No doctor’s note is required for standard sick days, unless required by law, repeated absence concerns, abuse concerns, extended absence, or Company policy. Sick days may also be used as mental health days. --- 23. Vacation Accrual Employees may accrue up to 5 additional vacation days per year. Vacation accrual is added every January 1. Accrual is based on time worked with the Company during the year. Example: If an employee starts in April, the employee worked approximately three-quarters of the year. The employee would receive three-quarters of the 5-day accrual. Three-quarters of 5 days equals 3.75 days, which rounds to 4 accrued days. Maximum vacation accrual: 30 days. --- 24. Sick Day Accrual Employees may accrue 2 additional sick days per year. Sick day accrual is added every January 1. Maximum sick day accrual: 15 days. Sick days may be used for: 1. Immediate illness. 2. Doctor’s appointments. 3. Mental health days. 4. Approved medical-related needs. For doctor’s appointments, proof is required and may include: 1. Screenshot of a calendar event; or 2. Shared Outlook calendar event. --- 25. PTO Carryover Employees may carry over the following from the previous year: 1. 5 vacation days. 2. 2 sick days. Carryover remains subject to maximum accrual limits, Company policy, and applicable law. --- 26. Bereavement Leave All eligible employees will receive 5 days of bereavement leave after either: 1. MAF Staffing Solutions has been in business for six months; or 2. The employee has completed the 90-day probationary period; whichever occurs later. Bereavement leave may be used for the death of a family member or friend. Bereavement leave may not be used for the death of a celebrity, public figure, or person with whom the employee did not have a genuine personal relationship, unless approved by the Company. --- 27. Parental Leave Employees are eligible for up to 6 months of parental leave, subject to Company policy, applicable law, and eligibility requirements. Months 1 Through 3 During months 1 through 3 of parental leave, employees will receive: 1. 100% salary. 2. 100% benefits. If an employee needs to clock in for any type of work during months 1 through 3, there will be no adjustment to parental leave pay or parental leave duration. Months 4 Through 6 During months 4 through 6 of parental leave, employees will receive: 1. 50% salary. 2. 100% benefits. If an employee needs to clock in for any type of work during months 4 through 6, they will be paid the remaining 50% of their salary for the day or days they work. If an employee works more than 50% of any given month during parental leave, MAF Staffing Solutions may determine that the employee has returned to work early, and parental leave may end. Parental leave shall be administered in compliance with applicable federal, state, and local law. --- 28. Healthcare and Additional Perk Programs After completion of the 90-day probationary period, eligible employees may receive access to healthcare benefits and additional Company perk programs. Healthcare and benefit access may depend on: 1. Employee classification. 2. Full-time or part-time status. 3. Plan eligibility rules. 4. Insurance provider rules. 5. Company policy. 6. Applicable law. 7. The Company being operational for the required period. If a benefit plan document conflicts with this Agreement, the formal plan document shall control to the extent required by law. --- 29. Separation and PTO Payout Upon separation from the Company, MAF Staffing Solutions will pay out all unused vacation days remaining for that year, subject to applicable law. Unused sick days will not be paid out upon separation unless required by applicable law. Commission payments earned before separation remain payable if they were: 1. Properly earned. 2. Properly documented. 3. Approved by the Company. 4. Funded by the employer or client. 5. Not subject to refund, dispute, chargeback, cancellation, or offset. This includes valid commissions banked during the first six months of business. --- 30. Mental Health and Wellness Sick days may be used as mental health days. MAF Staffing Solutions will reimburse payments and co-payments for licensed therapy sessions, with intervals as frequent as weekly. Therapy reimbursement limit: 1. Up to $150 per session. This amount will be reviewed and adjusted for inflation every April 1. Reimbursement requires proof of payment and confirmation that the provider is licensed. MAF Staffing Solutions will not require disclosure of private therapy notes, diagnosis information, treatment details, or confidential mental health information. --- 31. Gym Membership MAF Staffing Solutions will provide employees with a gym membership to a selected national gym chain. Selected National Gym Chain: ___________________________________________ The membership tier will be the lowest available tier that allows guests to work out for free. If an employee chooses not to use the selected gym, MAF Staffing Solutions will reimburse 25% of monthly or annual gym costs. Protein shakes, food, drinks, supplements, personal training, merchandise, premium upgrades, and other extras are not included unless approved in writing by the Company. --- 32. Nutritionist Benefit Outside of the health insurance plan, MAF Staffing Solutions will pay for one nutritionist visit per year, including any required lab work or blood work. Any medical, dietary, personal, or private information discussed during the visit remains private between the employee and the nutritionist. MAF Staffing Solutions only requires confirmation that the appointment was completed and the total cost of the appointment and related covered services. --- 33. Tuition Reimbursement MAF Staffing Solutions will offer tuition reimbursement for degrees, certifications, or courses related to an employee’s role or the goals of the Company. Maximum annual reimbursement: 1. $20,000 per year. This amount will be adjusted for inflation every April 1, using the inflation calculator provided by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics or another reasonable inflation source selected by the Company. Employees must attend in-state, in-county public institutions, including local community colleges, unless an exception is approved by the Company. Example: If an employee lives in Monmouth County, New Jersey, the employee should attend a public institution within that county when available. If an employee takes community college courses outside their county, reimbursement will be limited to 50% of the cost. Exceptions may be made if required coursework is not available at any in-state school. --- 34. Fully Covered Education Costs MAF Staffing Solutions will cover: 1. Books: 100%. 2. Lab supplies: 100%. Employees are responsible for: 1. Transportation. 2. Room and board. 3. Personal living expenses. 4. Meals. 5. Housing. 6. Relocation costs. 7. Non-approved school fees. Transportation or commuting costs for a necessary out-of-state college may be subsidized at 50%, subject to Company approval. --- 35. Meal and Snack Deliveries Monday Lunch Allowance Every Monday, MAF Staffing Solutions will provide lunch allowances as follows: 1. Directors and Department Heads: $50 allowance, not including tip. 2. All other staff: $30 allowance, not including tip. These allowance amounts will be tied to inflation. Lunch allowances may be modified, paused, or adjusted based on business needs, availability, provider limitations, budget, staffing, or Company operations. Monthly Snack Allowance Snacks will be ordered for all staff from Amazon every month. Each employee will have a monthly snack budget of: 1. $300 per month. This amount will be tied to inflation. Snack orders will be placed on the last Monday of every month. Possible future snack providers may include: 1. Felfel.com 2. SnackNation.com 3. SnackBoxUSA.com 4. Garden.co The Company may change, replace, pause, or modify snack providers based on availability, cost, dietary needs, business needs, or operational considerations. --- 36. Reimbursement Procedure All reimbursement requests must be submitted with proper proof. Proof may include: 1. Receipt. 2. Invoice. 3. Proof of payment. 4. Confirmation email. 5. Provider documentation. 6. Calendar confirmation. 7. School bill. 8. Therapy payment record. 9. Gym membership receipt. 10. Nutritionist invoice. 11. Any other documentation requested by the Company. The Company may deny reimbursement if the request is: 1. Unsupported. 2. Fraudulent. 3. Duplicated. 4. Excessive. 5. Unrelated to an approved benefit. 6. Submitted late. 7. Not permitted by this Agreement. 8. Not approved by the Company. --- 37. Confidentiality The Employee / Worker agrees to keep confidential all non-public Company information, including but not limited to: 1. Client information. 2. Employer information. 3. Candidate information. 4. Employee information. 5. Commission records. 6. Compensation information. 7. Staffing contracts. 8. Pricing information. 9. Business strategies. 10. Candidate pools. 11. Lead lists. 12. Internal documents. 13. Training materials. 14. Forms and templates. 15. Software access. 16. Private communications. The Employee / Worker shall not disclose confidential information to any unauthorized person or entity during or after employment or engagement with the Company. --- 38. Client, Candidate, and Employer Ownership All client, employer, and candidate information obtained through MAF Staffing Solutions belongs to the Company. The Employee / Worker may not use Company client lists, employer contacts, candidate pools, applicant information, staffing records, lead lists, or internal systems for personal gain or for the benefit of another company without written approval from the Company. --- 39. Non-Solicitation To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Employee / Worker agrees not to improperly solicit, divert, or interfere with the Company’s clients, employers, candidates, applicants, employees, contractors, recruiters, sourcers, sales representatives, or business relationships. This restriction shall apply only to the extent permitted by applicable law. --- 40. Company Property All Company documents, records, files, contracts, templates, forms, applicant records, candidate lists, client lists, employer contracts, marketing materials, databases, and internal business materials are the property of MAF Staffing Solutions. Upon separation or request by the Company, the Employee / Worker must return all Company property and delete, surrender, or transfer any Company materials in their possession, custody, or control. --- 41. Accuracy of Records The Employee / Worker agrees to provide accurate information regarding: 1. Hours worked. 2. Work performed. 3. Candidate submissions. 4. Client contacts. 5. Employer contracts. 6. Commission claims. 7. Expenses. 8. Reimbursement requests. 9. PTO requests. 10. Benefit claims. 11. Placement records. 12. First-90-day support records. False, misleading, incomplete, or fraudulent submissions may result in denial of payment, disciplinary action, termination, recovery of improper payments, and any other lawful remedy. --- 42. Taxes and Withholdings All wages, salaries, bonuses, commissions, reimbursements, and taxable benefits shall be subject to applicable taxes, withholdings, deductions, payroll requirements, and reporting obligations. Commission-based workers who are not classified as employees may be responsible for their own taxes, filings, insurance, and business expenses unless otherwise required by law. --- 43. No Guarantee of Commission Earnings The Company does not guarantee that any Employee / Worker will earn commissions, secure employer contracts, source candidates, complete placements, or receive any specific amount of commission income. Commission income depends on: 1. Actual work performed. 2. Company approval. 3. Proper documentation. 4. Employer or client payment. 5. Candidate acceptance. 6. Placement completion. 7. Compliance with this Agreement. 8. Business conditions. --- 44. Modification of Policies The Company may modify policies, procedures, benefits, reimbursement programs, commission procedures, documentation requirements, providers, forms, administrative procedures, and operational processes as needed for business, legal, financial, or operational reasons. Material changes to base compensation or commission percentages should be documented in writing. --- 45. This Agreement supersedes prior verbal or written discussions on these subjects, unless another written agreement signed by the Company provides otherwise. --- 46.
