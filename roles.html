📣 Sales Representative

Employer outreach, staffing contract opportunities, client relationship tracking, professional presentations and documented handoffs.

  • Comfortable contacting employers.
  • Organized call, email and lead records.
  • Clear understanding of what can and cannot be promised.

🔎 Candidate Sourcer

Talent pool research, candidate verification, organized submissions and recruiter handoffs.

  • Finds contactable candidates.
  • Tracks sources and candidate notes.
  • Submits candidates through approved processes.

🤝 Recruiter

Pre-screening, interview coordination, employer communication, placement support and first-90-day follow-up.

  • Strong communication skills.
  • Accurate screening records.
  • Follow-up with candidates and employers.

⭐ Internal Staffing

Support onboarding, communication, records, operations, retention, scheduling and documentation.

  • Reliable admin and communication habits.
  • Comfortable with email, spreadsheets and forms.
  • Strong attention to detail.

🏢 Department Head

Lead a team or department, support performance reviews, help with processes and keep work aligned with company goals.

🌍 Director

Provide leadership, strategy, accountability, team growth and operational support for company expansion.
