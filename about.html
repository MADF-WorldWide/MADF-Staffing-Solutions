About us

MADF WorldWide is built for detailed, professional, people-first hiring.

We help people show interest in recruitment, sourcing, sales, staffing support, employer communication, candidate support, leadership and internal operations roles across Europe.

What we do

MADF WorldWide supports recruitment work that can include candidate sourcing, employer outreach, pre-screening, placement support, internal staffing, onboarding communication and early-stage candidate follow-up.

Applicants are encouraged to provide as much detail as possible so owner Melissa Steele can assess how their experience, preferred roles, language skills and location may fit the company.
