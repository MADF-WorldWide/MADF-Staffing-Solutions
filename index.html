Agreement request

MADF Staffing Solutions / MADF WorldWide worker agreement overview

This template is for employment, compensation, commission, benefits, leave and worker terms between MADF Staffing Solutions / MADF WorldWide, represented by Melissa Steele, and the employee or worker. It should be completed with worker name, email, phone, address, role, department, start date and worker classification.

Purpose and compliance

The agreement sets out the relationship, duties, temporary salary structure, salary structure after six months, commissions, banked commissions, sales/sourcer/recruiter commission rights, probation, paid time off, leave, healthcare access, wellness benefits, tuition reimbursement, meal/snack allowances, confidentiality, client protection, records and professional conduct. Applicable law controls if any provision conflicts with law.

Position, duties and conduct

Workers may secure employer contracts, contact employers, build relationships, find candidate pools, submit candidates, pre-screen candidates, support employers through hiring, support candidates through the first 90 days, maintain records and assist operations. Workers must act professionally and must not misrepresent services, compensation, benefits, opportunities or company authority.

Compensation and commissions

The supplied template includes temporary first-six-month salary categories and post-six-month salary categories, Thursday payday language, annual cost-of-living raises, merit raise review intervals and uncapped/stackable commission concepts. Commission eligibility depends on qualifying activity, documentation, company approval, client payment and no refund, dispute, chargeback or reversal.

Role commission concepts

The template describes sales commission for employer staffing contracts, sourcer commission for candidate pools and recruiter commission for pre-screening, placement and first-90-day support. All commission records should include employer name, client contact, candidate details, source, submission date, recruiter, position filled, salary, contract date, proof of screening and support records.

Probation, PTO, leave and benefits

The template includes a 90-day probationary period, PTO access after company operating milestones, vacation and sick-day concepts, carryover limits, bereavement leave, parental leave, healthcare and benefit access, therapy reimbursement, gym membership or reimbursement, nutritionist benefit, tuition reimbursement and meal/snack allowances. These items must be checked against applicable law and formal plan documents.

Confidentiality, ownership and records

Company, client, employer, candidate, commission, compensation, staffing contract, pricing, business strategy, lead list, form, software and private communication information is confidential. Client, employer and candidate information obtained through MADF belongs to the company. False or incomplete records may result in denial of payment, discipline, termination or other lawful remedies.

Separation, modification and signature

The template includes separation language for vacation payout and earned commission handling, policy modification language, governing law blanks, severability and signature blocks for the company representative, employee/worker and optional witness.

Policies and agreements

Review applicant, privacy and website policies.

Attorney review required: these are practical website policy templates only, not legal advice. Recruitment, worker classification, privacy, wage and employment rules vary by country and location.

Privacy notice

When visitors submit forms, the website prepares an email to Melissa Steele. Information may include names, contact details, role preferences, location, work history, language skills, links and applicant statements. This information is used to review potential fit, respond to enquiries and maintain recruitment records.

Applicant consent

By submitting an application or enquiry, visitors consent to being contacted by email or phone about their submission and confirm that their information is accurate to the best of their knowledge.

Equal opportunity and respectful conduct

MADF WorldWide aims to review candidates professionally and respectfully. Applicants, workers, clients and representatives should not harass, discriminate, threaten, mistreat or misrepresent opportunities, compensation, benefits or services.

Data retention and deletion requests

Recruitment emails may be retained for application review, follow-up, compliance and operational records. To request correction or deletion of information, contact melissasteele462@outlook.com.

Website terms

Submitting a form does not guarantee employment, contract work, commission, interview, placement or hiring. Employment or engagement begins only when approved and documented by the company.

Cookies and social sharing

This static website does not intentionally set tracking cookies through a custom backend. Social share buttons open third-party sites or apps, which have their own policies.
